The CASEL 5 Framework

The foundational contribution of CASEL — established at their 1994 founding conference in Kalamazoo, Michigan — was the CASEL 5: five broad, interrelated areas that have defined the SEL industry for over three decades.

🪞

Self-Awareness

The ability to understand one's own emotions, thoughts, and values and how they influence behavior.

🎛️

Self-Management

The ability to regulate one's emotions, thoughts, and behaviors effectively across different situations.

🤝

Social Awareness

The ability to take the perspective of others and understand social and ethical norms for behavior.

💬

Relationship Skills

The ability to establish and maintain healthy and supportive relationships and navigate interpersonal situations.

⚖️

Responsible Decision-Making

The ability to make caring and constructive choices about personal behavior and social interactions.
