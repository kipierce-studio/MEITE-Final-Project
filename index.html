What's Inside

As the global education sector grapples with the rising mental health crisis in young students, Social-Emotional Learning has evolved from supplemental education into a multi-billion dollar technology-driven industry. This ePublication first provides an in-depth analysis of the Social-Emotional Learning industry, exploring the transition from traditional classroom instruction to a dynamic ecosystem where mental health, physical movement, and data analysis intersect. From the foundational Collaborative for Academic, Social, and Emotional Learning frameworks to the rise of Wellbeing-as-a-Service, this ePublication examines emerging trends for the future of Social-Emotional Learning, such as AI-driven analysis and the integration of physical activity with emotional health.

This ePublication next centers on a business profile of GoNoodle, a high-performing industry leader that has bridged the gap between physical activity and mental regulation for over 40 million children. The analysis will examine GoNoodle's business model, innovative features such as its classroom-to-home connectivity, as well as identify weaknesses and critical areas for growth. It will also examine key competitors and industry players for student social-emotional wellbeing. After, it will explore the unique connection between activity and Social-Emotional Learning. This ePublication will conclude with a roadmap for stakeholders to implement evidence-based tools that democratize mental health support and help drive long-term student success across diverse backgrounds, key focuses of GoNoodle's mission.
