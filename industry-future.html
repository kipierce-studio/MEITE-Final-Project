Three Predictions for the Future of SEL

The SEL industry is at an inflection point. The market is accelerating, the research is maturing, and AI is about to change everything.

🤖
Prediction 01

AI Will Personalize SEL at Scale

Near-term · 2025–2028

AI-driven emotional learning systems are being increasingly integrated within SEL frameworks — offering interactive simulations, role-playing environments, and real-time feedback. A 2024 systematic review found that VR, chatbots, gamification, and wearable devices present significant opportunities in the SEL space (Sethi & Jain, 2024).

What makes AI particularly powerful is its capacity for real-time personalization: tracking emotional patterns over time, flagging concerns to educators, and adapting support based on individual student needs.

Supporting Evidence
  • 2024 meta-analysis confirmed AI tools in SEL have significant positive potential (Sethi & Jain)
  • VR, chatbots, gamification, and wearables all identified as high-opportunity categories
  • Real-time personalization closes gap between one-size-fits-all curricula and individual needs
Critical Risk — Social Deskilling: Without intentional design promoting human interaction, researchers caution students may experience “social deskilling” — the erosion of empathy, negotiation, and non-verbal attunement. AI must amplify human connection, not substitute it.
📊
Prediction 02

SEL Outcomes Will Drive School Funding Formulas

Mid-term · 2026–2030

As federal investment increasingly ties dollars to measurable holistic outcomes, districts that cannot demonstrate SEL progress may find themselves at a funding disadvantage. The House of Representatives has already approved $260 million dedicated to measurable holistic child development outcomes.

Supporting Evidence
  • $260M House-approved funding tied to measurable holistic child outcomes
  • $87M in 2024 federal grants for data-supported SEL
  • 73% of 2024 SEL platform revenue from analytics and assessment tools
  • Panorama Education’s 42% accuracy improvement signaling market maturity
Implication for GoNoodle: This trend represents both an opportunity and a threat. GoNoodle’s strength is engagement, not analytics. To remain competitive, GoNoodle will need to invest significantly in reporting and measurement capabilities.
🌎
Prediction 03

The SEL Market Will Reach $21B — Driven by Global Expansion

Long-term · 2028–2033

The global SEL market reached $4.0 billion in 2024 and is projected to grow to $21.1 billion by 2033 (IMARC Group). International adoption across Europe, East Asia, and Latin America is expected to accelerate significantly as the research base becomes more universally recognized.

Supporting Evidence
  • $4.0B market (2024) → $21.1B projected (2033) — 5x growth in under a decade
  • CASEL’s established global partnerships provide distribution infrastructure
  • Post-pandemic mental health urgency is a global, not just American, phenomenon
Opportunity: GoNoodle’s physical movement model has inherently lower language barriers than text-heavy approaches — a natural international advantage.
